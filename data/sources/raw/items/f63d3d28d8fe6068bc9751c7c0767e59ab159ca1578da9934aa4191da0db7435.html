Notes

  • Cleave deals proc damage and is tagged as area of effect without applying spell effects.
  • Cleave is not blocked by spell shield.
  • Cleave does not trigger on attacks against structures.
  • Cleave will trigger even if the basic attack does not deal damage, such as if the target is invulnerable.
  • A single cast instance can cause a maximum of 10 Cleave splashes.
  • Crescent deals area damage and thus will trigger spell effects.
  • Crescent is blocked by spell shield.
  • Crescent is an auto-targeted effect.
  • Crescent's center is offset by 100 units in the direction the caster is facing.
  • Crescent's cast breaks stealth.
  • Crescent has a cast time equal to the user's attack windup.
    • If Tiamat's item slot is changed during this cast time, the area of effect spell will not occur.(bug)
  • Crescent goes on cooldown at the end of its cast.

Trivia

  • In Babylonian mythology, Tiamat was the first dragon and was slain to make the heavens, earth, and man.
    • Tiamat is also the name of the five headed dragon goddess in the Dungeons & Dragons tabletop RPG.
      • Tiamat building into three items ( Profane Hydra, Ravenous Hydra, Titanic Hydra) borrow their namesake from the Hydra, another many-headed beast, also alludes to this.
  • In the V1.0.0.115 April Fools' Day patch, Tiamat's name was jokingly given the following update to reference Tamat, the name of a Rioter.
    • Tiamat has been renamed to Tamat.
