Patch History

V14.19
  • Ability power reduced to 25 from 30.
V14.1
  • Unique passive renamed to Grievous Wounds from Cursed.
  • No longer builds into Chemtech Putrifier.
V13.7
  • New Effect: Now applies Grievous Wounds even if the damage dealt was completely mitigated by a shield.
V13.1b
  • Grievous Wounds strength increased to 40% from 25%.
  • Ability power reduced to 30 from 35.
V12.11
  • Grievous Wounds strength reduced to 25% from 30%.
  • Ability power increased to 35 from 30.
V12.10
  • Grievous Wounds strength reduced to 30% from 40%.
V11.8
  • New Effect: Is now called out in chat to notify when the item is purchased.
V11.1
  • Grievous Wounds duration increased to 3 seconds from 2.
V10.23
  • Updated icon.
  • New:
    • Recipe: Amplifying Tome + 365 = 800.
    • Stats: 30 ability power.
    • Unique Passive - Cursed: Dealing magic damage against champions applies 40% Grievous Wounds for 2 seconds.
    • Builds Into: Chemtech Putrifier, Morellonomicon.
  • Old:
    • Recipe: Ruby Crystal + Amplifying Tome + 765 = 1600.
    • Stats: 20 ability power, 200 health.
    • Unique Passive - Touch of Death: +15 magic penetration.
    • Builds Into: Morellonomicon.
V9.2
  • Total cost increased to 1600 from 1500.
    • Combine cost increased to 765 from 665.
  • Ability power reduced to 20 from 25.
V8.17
  • No longer builds into Bloodletter's Veil, due to the item being removed from the game.
V8.16 - Start of the experimental game mode Nexus Blitz
  • Re-added back to the recipe for Bloodletter's Veil.
V8.12 - End of the Curse of the Drowned event (June 18th, 2018)
  • Removed from recipe for Bloodletter's Veil, due to the item being removed from the game.
V8.11 - Start of the Curse of the Drowned event
  • Added to recipe for Bloodletter's Veil.
V8.4 - Added
  • Recipe: Ruby Crystal + Amplifying Tome + 665 = 1500.
  • Stats: +25 ability power, +200 health.
  • Unique Passive - Touch of Death: +15 magic penetration.
  • Builds into: Morellonomicon.
  • Availability: All maps.
