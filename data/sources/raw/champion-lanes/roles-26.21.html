    How tiers and the ranking are set
    • Tier S wins at least 53% and is picked in at least 4% of games
    • Tier A wins at least 51.5% and is picked in at least 2.5% of games
    • Tier B wins at least 49.5%
    • Tier C wins at least 47.5%
    • Tier D wins less than 47.5%

    Win rate is wins out of games; pick rate is the share of the view's games the champion played; ban rate is the share in which it was banned. A champion needs 100 games in the view (in the position, when one is picked) for a place and a tier, and takes the first tier line it meets. Below that, a rate can swing by several points on a handful of games, so it stays grey and unranked. Difficulty uses the bands of Riot's own champion pages (Low 0 to 3, Medium 4 to 7, High 8 to 10) on Riot's 0 to 10 rating.
