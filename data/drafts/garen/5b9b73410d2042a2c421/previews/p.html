Garen P · Perseverance

L2 · Priority B
Perseverance regenerates a portion of Garen's maximum health while active. Champion, epic monster or turret damage, enemy ability hits, and enemy summoner spells can temporarily interrupt it.
Counterplay: Use safe opportunities to pressure him so he cannot freely recover health between fights.
