Hwei Q · Subject: Disaster

L2 · Priority A
Subject: Disaster opens his damage choices; casting a choice exits the mood. QQ — Devastating Fire: a fireball explodes on the first enemy hit or at maximum range, dealing nearby magic damage with a target maximum-health component. QW — Severing Bolt: delayed area lightning deals magic damage, amplified against isolated or immobilized enemies based on their missing health. QE — Molten Fissure: a delayed line of explosions leaves lava that repeatedly deals magic damage and slows enemies. Wash Brush cancels the mood without its ability cost or cooldown.
Counterplay: Dodge the fireball and lightning area, leave the lava path, and take extra care against lightning while low on health or immobilized.
